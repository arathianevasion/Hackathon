# Post-Enrollment Client Engagement

How insurance Agents keep their Clients covered and engaged after the sale, with an AI Assistant doing the work on the Agent's behalf.

## Language

### People

**Agent**:
The licensed human insurance agent who owns a book of Clients.
_Avoid_: Broker, producer, user, AI agent

**Assistant**:
The AI that works on an Agent's behalf to engage that Agent's Clients.
_Avoid_: AI agent, bot, copilot

**Household**:
The people on one Marketplace application, whether or not each is enrolled.
_Avoid_: Family, application, case

**Household member**:
Any person in a Household.
_Avoid_: Member (ambiguous), dependent, enrollee

**Client**:
The primary Household member, the person the Agent and Assistant deal with.
_Avoid_: Customer, consumer, member, applicant

### Coverage

**Enrollment**:
A Household member's coverage in a specific plan.
_Avoid_: Member, policy, subscription

**Data Matching Issue (DMI)**:
An ACA Marketplace finding that a Household member's application info doesn't match trusted data sources, which must be resolved with supporting documents by a deadline or risk losing coverage or subsidies.
_Avoid_: DMR, data matching requirement

**Document**:
A file a Client uploads through the portal to resolve a DMI.
_Avoid_: Attachment, upload, proof

### Working with the Client

**Consent record**:
The documented, unrescinded permission from a Client that authorizes the Agent, and so the Assistant, to take a given kind of action.
_Avoid_: Consent form, authorization, permission

**Thread**:
The single running text conversation between a Client and the Assistant, which the Agent can also post in.
_Avoid_: Chat, conversation, inbox

### Handover to the Agent

**Escalation**:
A handover of a Client's situation from the Assistant to the Agent that requires the Agent to act.
_Avoid_: Alert, notification (a Notification needs no action)

**Notification**:
An informational message from the Assistant to the Agent that requires no action.
_Avoid_: Alert, escalation
