# Post-Enrollment Client Engagement

How insurance Agents keep their Clients covered and engaged after the sale, with an AI Assistant doing the work on the Agent's behalf.

## Language

**Agent**:
The licensed human insurance agent who owns a book of Clients.
_Avoid_: Broker, producer, user, AI agent

**Assistant**:
The AI that works on an Agent's behalf to engage that Agent's Clients.
_Avoid_: AI agent, bot, copilot

**Client**:
A person in an Agent's book of business.
_Avoid_: Customer, consumer, member (a Member is an enrollment, not a person)

**Data Matching Issue (DMI)**:
An ACA Marketplace finding that a Member's application info doesn't match trusted data sources, which the Client must resolve with supporting documents by a deadline or risk losing coverage or subsidies.
_Avoid_: DMR, data matching requirement

**Member**:
A Client's enrollment in a specific plan; a Client can have several, one per line of business.
_Avoid_: Enrollee, policy, subscriber
