# Product standards

Product level: the product's taste, in this repo and changed by PR. Read by triage, workers and verifiers at the point of use.

## Always ask

Things that must always come back to the Artisan. Only the Artisan changes this list (setup or a retro lesson).
- Client-facing wording: any text the Assistant sends or words on the upload page
- Security and personal data: Portal link rules, masking, what the model sees, the outbound filter
- Escalation and nudge rules: triggers, the nudge schedule, what the Assistant may do on its own
